# Privacy Policy - Paws & Hooves Uploader

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Paws & Hooves Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Paws & Hooves (https://www.youtube.com/@PawsAndHoovesDaily). It uploads the channel's own videos to that channel through the YouTube Data API.

What data is accessed: The app uses the YouTube upload permission (youtube.upload) only to publish videos to the owner's own channel. It does not read, collect, store, sell or share any personal data of viewers or other users.

Google user data: Data received from Google APIs is used only to upload videos, and its use adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. No Google user data is transferred to third parties.

YouTube: By using this app, the channel owner is bound by the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms). Google's Privacy Policy applies: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Revoking access: Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
